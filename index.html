Play without the fuss

A desktop first.
A game library close by.

Ludic is being built for people who want a proper PC desktop and a simpler path to Linux gaming. Use a mouse and keyboard, keep your familiar workflow, then jump into Steam or your other game libraries.

Get to know Ludic ↘

Ludic uses an image-based Fedora Atomic system. Updates arrive together, and the previous system version stays available if you need to roll back.

Take it for a spin

Try the desktop
before you commit.

The planned live preview will start Ludic from a USB drive or virtual machine. Look around, open the apps and see how the desktop feels. It has no installer and makes no changes to your computer on its own.
